# Типы шкал зависимости от ИИ (протокол §13)

Тип определяется по **содержанию пунктов**, а не по названию шкалы. Решаете вы; черновик ИИ — над строкой решения.

| Тип | Пункты про… |
| --- | --- |
| T1 аддиктивная | компоненты поведенческой зависимости: поглощённость, изменение настроения, толерантность, абстиненция, конфликт или негативные последствия, потеря контроля, рецидив |
| T2 функциональная | опору на ИИ в задачах и мышлении: учёба, письмо, решения; трудно работать или думать без ИИ |
| T3 эмоциональная | эмоциональную опору: поддержка, утешение, компания, привязанность, тоска без ИИ как без близкого |
| T4 смешанная или неясно | сумма пунктов разных типов без явного большинства; или пунктов нет и судить не по чему |

**Строка решения:**
- `=` — согласен с черновиком (и для суммы, и для подшкал);
- `T1`…`T4` — свой тип суммы, подшкалы как в черновике;
- `T4; Instrumental Dependency=T2; Relationship Dependency=T3` — сумма и подшкалы (имя подшкалы как в черновике);
- `?` — пока не решил.

Почему это важно: главный модератор — тип шкалы; в сравнение T1/T2/T3 идут только шкалы и подшкалы, которым вы дали тип T1–T3, а T4 показываются отдельно.

- Пунктов: 24 / 5 / 6 / 7; подшкалы: Total composite used. Two conceptual facets: behavioral dysregulation (PD1–PD3, 3 items) and relational salience/emotionally consequential reliance (PD4–PD6, 3 items). In a two-factor CFA the facets correlated 0.9998, so a single factor was retained.
- Пункты (23, показаны первые 15):

  - PED2. When I cannot use my AI companion, I feel irritable or anxious.

  - PED4. I feel that my AI companion really understands me as a person.
  - PED5. I have developed a special emotional attachment to my AI companion and increasingly need it to maintain my emotional stability.
  - PED6. Sometimes I prioritize interacting with my AI companion over communicating with real people.
  - I find myself wanting to use AI chatbots more and more often, even if there is no particular reason for it.

  - Using AI chatbots has started to affect my performance at school/work.
  - I have been interacting with AI chatbots for much longer than I had originally planned.

  - PACU1. I spent a lot of time thinking about AI chatbots or planned use of AI chatbots.
  - PACU2. I felt an urge to use AI chatbots more and more.

**ИИ:** сумма T1 (аддиктивная) · основание: items · Most printed items in this family follow Bergen/Griffiths components: salience (PACU1), tolerance (PACU2), relapse/failed cut-down (PACU3, PED1), withdrawal (PACU4, PED2), conflict/negative consequences (PACU5) and loss of control (PED3). The family is T1 by majority. Two variants depart from this: PED4–PED6 are emotional-attachment items (T3), and some AID inventory items are about functional reliance (T2).

### 4 · GAIDS

**Generative AI Dependency Scale** (и ещё 5 вариантов названия)

- Отчёты (7): R2164, R2781, R1476, R0918, R1201, R1060, R2241
- Источник: Goh, Adalia & Hartanto, Andree & M. Majeed, Nadyanna. (2025). Generative Artificial Intelligence Dependency: Scale Development, Validation, and its Motivational, Behavioral, and Psychological Correlates. Computers in Human Behavior Reports. 20. 10084
- Пунктов: 11 / 9 / Not reported; подшкалы: Not reported. The scale is described as covering frequency of use, emotional attachment, and perceived necessity of AI for academic tasks. The abstract mentions 'AI opinion, AI study, and AI creativity' without defining them. Total score used: higher scores mean greater dependence; observed range 11
- Пункты (4):
  - My decisions are mostly influenced by generative artificial intelligence
  - I feel a sense of disconnection when I cannot access generative artificial intelligence.
  - I get frustrated or irritable when I am unable to use generative AI
  - My decisions are often influenced by generative AI

**ИИ:** сумма T1 (аддиктивная) · основание: items · The 11-item total is built on Goodman's behavioural-addiction model. 8 of its 11 items fall in addiction-component subscales: withdrawal (4) and negative consequences (4). The printed withdrawal items describe irritability and disconnection when the AI can't be accessed. The 3 cognitive-preoccupation items, judged from their one printed item, read as functional reliance in decision-making. So the majority type is T1, with a minority of T2-like content.
- подшкала «Cognitive preoccupation (3 items; one report calls it 'cognitive distress')»: T2 — The name is an addiction component (salience), but the only printed item ('My decisions are mostly/often influenced by generative AI') is about handing decisions over to AI, which is functional dependence. This rests on one item, so it is uncertain; if typed by name alone it would be T1.
- подшкала «Negative consequences (4 items)»: T1 — Named after an addiction component (conflict/negative consequences). No item text is printed, so the evidence is the subscale name only.
- подшкала «Withdrawal (4 items)»: T1 — The items describe withdrawal-like irritability and disconnection when the AI can't be used, e.g., 'I get frustrated or irritable when I am unable to use generative AI'. Typed from item content.
- Решающие пункты: «I get frustrated or irritable when I am unable to use generative AI» | «I feel a sense of disconnection when I cannot access generative artificial intelligence.» | «My decisions are mostly influenced by generative artificial intelligence»

### 8 · IAT_ADAPTED

**AI Dependency Scale (ADS-R)** (и ещё 3 вариантов названия)

- Отчёты (4): R2935, R1025, R0953, R0953
- Источник: Developed for this study; item pool adapted from Young (1998) Internet Addiction Test, informed by the psychometric analysis of Widyanto & McMurran (2004). No DOI printed.
- Пунктов: 10 / 15 / 20 / 7; подшкалы: None; total score only. Content mapping: time management/functional interference (IAT items 1, 6, 8, 16, 17); withdrawal/social repercussions (13, 18, 20); reality substitution (10); anticipation/preoccupation (11)
- Пункты (4):

  - I often find myself relying on AI tools more than intended when completing coursework.

**ИИ:** сумма T4 (смешанная или неясно) · основание: items · This family groups different instruments whose content differs. The 4 printed items come from the ADS-R and the 7-item coursework ADS. - подшкала «Y_AutonomyLoss: Autonomy Erosion and Life Proxy (ADS-R, 6 items)»: T2 — The name describes handing judgement and life tasks over to AI. The printed item 'can't work/think without AI' fits difficulty functioning without AI. Item-to-subscale assignment is not reported.

### 10 · AIDEP22

**AI Dependence Scale for Chinese Undergraduates (AIDep-22)** (и ещё 1 вариантов названия)

- Отчёты (3): R2703, R2687, R4336
- Источник: Wu et al. (2026) (no DOI printed in text)
- Пунктов: 22; подшкалы: Emotional dependence; functional dependence; cognitive dependence; loss of control (item counts per subscale not reported). The global score is the mean of the items, and higher scores mean greater dependence.
- Пункты (29, показаны первые 15):
  - I feel uneasy or insecure without the help of AI.
  - Using AI to assist my studies makes me feel calm and relaxed.
  - I would feel anxious and frustrated if I couldn't use AI for my studies.
  - Whenever I encounter a difficult problem, my first instinct is to hope AI can help me; otherwise, I feel tense.
  - Interacting with AI via chat prompts is a pleasant experience that makes me feel supported.
  - I delegate almost all my academic tasks to AI because it performs them faster and better than I do.
  - I rely on AI's functions, including providing answers and writing suggestions, to improve my learning efficiency.
  - I can no longer manage complex academic tasks without the assistance of AI.
  - I prefer to delegate information gathering and content organization to AI rather than doing it entirely myself.
  - In my studies, I excessively rely on the ready-made answers or solutions provided by AI.
  - I feel inadequate in my studies without the support of AI.
  - I often ask AI for answers directly without thinking for myself first.
  - After using AI, my ability to independently analyze and solve problems has declined.
  - When faced with an academic challenge, I rely on AI instead of attempting to think it through on my own.
  - After long-term use of AI, I have become less inclined to think independently, preferring AI to do the work.

**ИИ:** сумма T4 (смешанная или неясно) · основание: items · The 22 items split about evenly. Functional (6) and cognitive (5) dependence items are about handing study work and thinking to AI, which is T2 (11 items). Loss-of-control items (6) and the emotional-dependence items (5) cover compulsive use, failed attempts to cut down, withdrawal-like distress and mood modification, which is T1-like (11 items).
