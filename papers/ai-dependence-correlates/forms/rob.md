# Risk of bias: six items adapted from AXIS (protocol §14)

Downes et al. (2016), BMJ Open 6(12), e011458, https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-011458. Each item is rated **yes / no / unclear** per sample. The AI drafts each rating with a supporting quote (page, table or sentence); R1 verifies and decides. No summary score.

| # | Item | Yes | No | Unclear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sampling | probability sample, census of a defined population (all students of a cohort, all employees of a unit) or a full cohort | convenience, snowball, online recruitment, crowdsourcing panels (Prolific, MTurk, Credamo, Wenjuanxing), quota panels | method not described |
| 2 | Response | response rate ≥ 60%, or non-responders compared with responders | response rate < 60% with no comparison | not reported (typical for online links) |
| 3 | Validated AI-dependence measure | published development or validation study of the instrument (factor structure and reliability), or a validation reported in the same paper | ad hoc items without psychometric evidence; single items | instrument not named |
| 4 | Reliability of the AI-dependence measure in the sample | α or ω ≥ .70 reported for this sample | α or ω < .70 | not reported |
| 5 | Validated correlate measures | every correlate used in the analysis is measured with a published instrument | at least one correlate measured with ad hoc items | instruments not named |
| 6 | Data quality | attention checks, careless-responding screening or a stated rule for invalid responses | the report states that no screening was done | not reported |

Items 1, 3 and 6 enter the analysis as moderators and sensitivity filters (protocol §15b–c, §17).
